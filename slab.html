Our story

How this all started…

It's funny how two jokesters joked their way into starting a business. This whole thing was never really the plan.

Chris was heads-down building his insurance agency around the Greater Sacramento area, learning the hard way what worked and what didn't, and swapping strategies with other local owners. Brandon, who runs the Failure to Abundance podcast, kept watching him help these entrepreneurs and finally dared him: "If you think you know so much, why don't you come on the podcast and prove it?"

"Uh oh… I think we accidentally started a business."

Chris distilled years of grinding into a no-fluff presentation. People asked them to speak to their groups, so they put on a live event to see what would happen. It sold out. The next one sold out faster. That's what grew into The Average Joe's MBA.

We're not academics — we're practitioners. Brandon and Chris still run their own businesses every single day, right here in Northern California.
